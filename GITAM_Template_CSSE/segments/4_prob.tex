\begin{frame}{Problem Statement}
\begin{exampleblock}{Core Problem Statement}
\large
Visually impaired individuals face acute challenges in independent navigation, particularly in unstructured Indian road settings characterized by potholes, mixed traffic, blocked footpaths, and chaotic intersections. 

\vspace{0.3cm}
Current assistive technologies either depend on \textbf{expensive specialized hardware} (LiDAR, smart glasses, ultrasonic canes) or offer \textbf{narrow functionality} without contextual scene interpretation. 

\vspace{0.3cm}
There is an urgent requirement for an \textbf{affordable, smartphone-based assistive navigation system} integrating real-time edge object detection, AI-powered scene understanding, turn-by-turn GPS navigation, OCR text reading, and natural voice interaction to ensure safe, autonomous mobility.
\end{exampleblock}
\end{frame}
