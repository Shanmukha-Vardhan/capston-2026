\begin{frame}{Problem Statement}
{\large\textbf{\textcolor{MyUnivGreen}{Formal Problem Statement}}}\\[8pt]

Visually impaired individuals face acute challenges in independent navigation, particularly in unstructured Indian road environments characterized by potholes, mixed traffic, blocked footpaths, and complex intersections.\\[8pt]

Current assistive technologies suffer from two fundamental shortcomings:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hardware Dependency:} Reliance on expensive, specialized hardware (LiDAR, ultrasonic canes, smart glasses) that are unaffordable for the vast majority of users in developing countries.
    \item \textbf{Functional Limitations:} Existing tools detect proximity but cannot semantically explain the environment or provide audio-guided turn-by-turn navigation.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{Goal:} Develop an \textbf{affordable, smartphone-based assistive navigation system} integrating real-time edge AI detection (YOLO), contextual scene understanding (Gemini VLM), turn-by-turn audio GPS routing, OCR text recognition, and conversational voice interaction.
\end{frame}
